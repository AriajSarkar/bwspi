## Interpretation boundaries

`HashSet`/`BTreeSet` deduplicate values, whereas BWSPI and `Vec` retain duplicates. They are included for contains/insert reference, not as semantic replacements. `Vec::binary_search` includes the one-time pre-sort only in the sort rows, not in lookup timing. Rust's `sort_unstable` and stable `sort` are the standard-library sorting baselines.
